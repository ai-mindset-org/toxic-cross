# Krest – method and interface update – 2026-10-20

This update revisits the original four-direction practice and the product. The result is a daily overview, explicit time horizons, inspectable source types, reversible exclusions and an incoming-change journal. Research informs these design choices. It does not validate Krest's weights or infer a person's psychological state.

## Original practice

The original practice reviews commitments, evidence from past experience, available resources and plausible future scenarios together. The older visualization also used a fixed quarter-weight flip and timed recoil. These numerical effects were authored visual rules. This search did not locate an independent evaluation of Krest or a standardized instrument with these axes. The exact-name search returned our own product and unrelated material; our pages are excluded as independent evidence.

The current board retains the questions, source inspection, time windows and four-direction field. Scheduled context, source arithmetic, explicit check-ins and temporary reframe previews remain separate. Reviewing all directions is available as a reflection sequence. The software does not establish that every situation requires a simultaneous four-part intervention. Work duration and pacing remain personal decisions.

## Applied contract

1. **Overview:** accepted plan items retain explicit confirmation and date. Suggested actions are labelled drafts. Missing or expired plan sources keep their receipt.
2. **Timeline:** exact event timestamps establish scheduled intervals and phases. Relative offsets and scope estimates are labelled separately. Elapsed scheduled time does not establish attendance or completion.
3. **Inspection:** hover, keyboard focus and the detail drawer reveal type, source, time basis, weight and freshness. Optional source colours retain text labels.
4. **Changes:** observed additions, removals, revisions and board exclusions show their model-weight delta. They establish no causal or emotional effect on the person.
5. **Exclusions:** hiding removes a record from this board's active calculation. Restore uses retained identity; upstream calendars and task systems retain their records. A vanished upstream observation cannot be fabricated by restore.
6. **Check-ins:** personal reports and notes use a separate journal. Reminder controls are explicit. An unrated event never silently becomes a neutral report.
7. **Reframe:** questions and a next step leave source facts intact. A temporary weight preview retains its visible multiplier and original baseline.
