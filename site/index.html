where the cross comes from.

The “toxic cross” model is Seva Ustinov’s idea, discussed on 15 September 2026. Krest is an attempt to test it hands-on with live data.

Obligations, resources, past and future share one field. The dot shows declared arithmetic. Weights and reframe previews are experimental choices with no psychological calibration.

method & limits ↗

your sources stay yours.

The public demo uses invented records. Your own calendar and task files belong in ignored local files. Calendar reads are read-only. Check-ins are a separate journal; a private-board connection enables ratings sync with a separate off switch. Review real day files and screenshots before sharing.
