Four directions make the load visible.

Each fact becomes a weighted block. Half-lives are 1 day for situational context, 7 for weekly context and 45 for global context.

↑ obligations
How many people are watching, and what do they expect?
↓ resources
Money, time and energy available to you.
← past
Where has something similar failed before?
→ future
The fan of bad scenarios you can imagine.

Dot = (future − past, obligations − resources), normalized for the screen. The default signal appears when obligations exceed 3 × resources.

Flip one arm and its neighbours return about 75% of the removed weight over 26 seconds. Flip all four together and the change holds. This is an illustrative rule of the model, with no psychological calibration.

The original post connects Beck’s cognitive triad, Lazarus’s appraisal of demands against resources and Hobfoll’s loss spiral. Krest tests that combined picture through declared source weights.

Make it yours: rename the arms, choose sources and their scales, half-lives and the signal threshold, then connect one source. configure → · read the method ↗
